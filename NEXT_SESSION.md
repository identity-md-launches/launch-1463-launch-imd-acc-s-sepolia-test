## imd/acc in brief
Trading cashback paid in **staked IMD (sIMD)**, inspired by TokenWorks' btc/acc. A project sends **0.25%–1% of each trade** (its choice; PLEA uses 0.5%), out of fees it already charges, to the trader as sIMD through one shared, ownerless **`Stacker.credit(trader, imdAmount)`**: it pulls the IMD from the project and calls `sIMD.deposit(amount, trader)`, so the vault mints shares straight to the trader. No new token, no cut. Every `Stacked(project, trader, imd, shares)` event is a record; **points** = 1 per IMD stacked, counted only for **listed** projects inside their listing periods. A **fee guard** stops owners from farming points. Listing is decided by the ownerless **ProjectRegistry**: a project's owner applies (anyone, for a project without an `owner()`), anyone challenges, an IdentityMD oracle panel answers yes or no through Intake v2 against 8 written rules (version 3), and a challenge bond protects projects from spam.
